# Artwork brief

Artwork for Tamer, a World of Warcraft: Forever addon that shows a Hunter what their pet should learn next and which beast teaches it. Everything here is for our artist.

CurseForge requires the logo to be original. It can't use a game's logo or a generic game graphic, or copy another project's logo.

## What's needed

| Asset | Size | Needed for | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project logo | 1024x1024 PNG, square | The CurseForge page and search results | Required |
| In-game icon | The same art, simplified, 512x512 PNG with a transparent background | The round window portrait, the AddOns list | Recommended |
| Minimap icon | 512x512 PNG with a transparent background, readable at about 20 pixels | The minimap button | Nice to have |
| Banner | 1280x400 PNG, with room for the title on the right | The top of the CurseForge description | Nice to have |

Send PNGs at these sizes. They go in the project's `art/` folder as `logo.png`, `icon.png`, `minimap.png` and `banner.png`, and the in-game versions get converted to the format and sizes the game needs.

## Art direction

- **The idea:** the moment of taming. A Hunter's gloved hand held out, palm open and steady, and a wild beast lowering its head to sniff it. The beast hasn't decided yet, and the hand isn't grabbing. It's trust about to happen, which is what taming is.
- **The beast:** a wolf or a bear. They're the pets most Hunters start with, and both have a strong, readable head shape. Pick whichever looks best; it doesn't have to match across pieces.
- **The mood:** calm, patient, a little wild. Quiet respect between the two, not cute and not fierce.
- **The look:** hand-painted fantasy art with chunky shapes and bold, readable silhouettes. Forest green and worn leather brown, with warm gold light where the snout meets the hand, as the one bright spot.
- **Readable when small:** one clear subject, high contrast, and no fine detail that gets lost at icon size. The hand and the snout should still read at 64x64 pixels.
- **Fits with Fireside Tales:** our other addon uses the same hand-painted style in amber and blue-black. Tamer should feel like a sibling, in its own greens and browns.

## Things to avoid

- World of Warcraft logos, the Hunter class crest, faction crests, or text from the game.
- The game's own ability icons or spell effects, such as Beast Training or the Tame Beast channel.
- Blizzard characters, such as Rexxar, or anything copied from official art.
- Other addons' logos. Several other Forever pet addons exist, so it shouldn't look like theirs.
- A hand that grabs, a leash, or a cage. Taming here is trust, not force.
- Any text in the art. The name is added separately.

## Each piece

**Project logo:**

A square icon of a gloved Hunter's hand reaching in from the lower left, palm open, and a wolf's head (or a bear's) coming in from the upper right, lowering its snout to sniff the palm. Only the hand, wrist and a bit of leather sleeve show, with no face or body. A soft gold glow where the snout and palm almost touch. Framed in a round, worn leather badge. Forest green and leather brown, with the gold glow as the one bright spot. High contrast, readable at small sizes. No text, no logos.

**In-game icon:**

The same idea simplified: the open palm and the beast's snout, close up, centered on a transparent background. Keep everything well inside a centered circle, because the game crops the corners.

**Minimap icon:**

Just the snout touching an open palm, as a bold silhouette with a faint gold glow, on a transparent background. Extremely simple, with strong contrast, readable at 20 pixels.

**Banner:**

A wide scene at dusk in a forest clearing: a Hunter kneeling, seen from behind and to the side, one hand held out low and open. A wild bear or wolf steps out of the trees toward them, head lowered, just about to sniff the hand. Warm gold light falls between them. Keep the Hunter generic, with no face shown and no recognizable race armor or character, and leave open space on the right side for the title. Hand-painted fantasy art, forest greens and leather browns. No text, no logos.
